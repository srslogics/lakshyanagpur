Student and Parent Consent and Terms

Lakshya Institute, Sadar, Nagpur · Version student-parent-v1-2026-08-15 · Effective 15 August 2026

Important: These terms record informed consent and reasonable responsibilities. They do not waive any non-excludable statutory right, prevent a complaint to a competent authority, or excuse unlawful conduct.

1. Who may accept

An adult student may accept these terms for themselves. If the student is below 18, a parent or lawful guardian must accept the contractual and data-processing terms. A minor’s acceptance alone is not treated as a binding contract. A student using the Student portal confirms that their parent or lawful guardian has authorised that use where required.

2. Information supplied

The student and parent or guardian confirm that identity, age, address, contact, school, health, accessibility and academic information supplied to the Institute is accurate to the best of their knowledge. Material changes should be communicated promptly. Deliberate falsification, impersonation or use of another person’s account may result in access suspension and lawful corrective action.

3. Educational services and outcomes

The Institute will provide the course, teaching support, academic administration and student services described in the applicable admission offer and published schedule, subject to reasonable timetable, faculty, room and delivery-mode changes. Examination ranks, marks, admission, scholarships and career outcomes depend on factors outside the Institute’s control. No particular result is guaranteed. This does not limit the Institute’s responsibility to provide paid services with reasonable care and skill.

4. Attendance and biometric-device records

Attendance may be recorded through classroom registers, the attendance application and an on-premises biometric device. A student must use only their own identifier. Attendance records may be made available to the student, parent or guardian and authorised Institute personnel for academic, safety and administrative purposes.

The ERP is intended to receive the mapped student identifier, event date and time, and relevant device metadata. Fingerprint images or templates are not to be uploaded to the ERP unless the Institute first provides a separate notice describing the purpose, storage, retention, security and lawful basis. A device timestamp records a check-in event and does not by itself prove continuous presence for an entire class.

5. Academic records

The Institute may schedule examinations, publish timetables, record marks, assignments, attendance and feedback, and make these records available through the student and parent portals. Provisional entries may be corrected after verification. Users must not manipulate, scrape, sell or disclose another student’s records. Questions about marks should be raised within the notified review period.

6. Fees, cancellation and refunds

The applicable fee schedule, instalments, due dates, scholarships, concessions and refund or cancellation terms are supplied separately. Payments should be supported by an Institute receipt or verifiable electronic reference. Nothing in these terms permits retention of money contrary to applicable law or the written refund policy. Cancelled, dropped or forfeited enrolments must be recorded accurately so that receivables, refunds, credits and balances are not misrepresented.

7. Account security and availability

Users must protect passwords, use only their own account, sign out from shared devices and report suspected compromise promptly. The Institute may temporarily suspend access to investigate misuse or protect data. Maintenance, connectivity failure, power failure, third-party disruption or a cybersecurity response may temporarily affect availability. Reasonable steps will be taken to restore service and preserve records, but uninterrupted access is not promised.

8. Personal data and purposes

The Institute may process identity and contact details, admission and course records, attendance timestamps, fee and receipt records, assignments, examinations, communications, inventory issues, device and session logs, safety records and information voluntarily supplied for enrolment, teaching, academic administration, attendance, safety, accounting, authentication, parent communication, grievances, legal compliance, security and service improvement.

Access is limited to authorised personnel and service providers who require it for an authorised purpose. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, a competent authority, child-safety duties, an emergency, or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Records will be retained only as reasonably necessary for their stated purpose, accounting or education requirements, safeguarding, dispute resolution or legal obligations, and then deleted or anonymised under the Institute’s retention process.

For a child, the parent or lawful guardian provides verifiable consent where required. The Institute will not use a child’s data for targeted advertising or behavioural monitoring and will not process it in a manner likely to harm the child’s well-being.

9. Communications and publicity

Necessary service communications may be sent through the ERP, telephone, SMS, email or WhatsApp at registered contact details. These include timetable, attendance, examination, assignment, fee, safety, account-security and grievance messages. This consent does not include promotional marketing or publicity use of a student’s photograph or video. Those optional choices must be requested separately, and refusal will not affect academic services.

10. Safety and emergencies

The parent or guardian authorises reasonable first aid, contact with emergency services and urgent transport when delay may materially risk the student’s health or safety, while the Institute makes reasonable efforts to contact the registered guardian. This does not authorise non-emergency medical treatment without appropriate consent. Known allergies, medical risks and accessibility needs should be disclosed through the Institute’s confidential channel.

11. Child protection

The Institute maintains zero tolerance for sexual abuse, harassment, exploitation, grooming, bullying, retaliation and corporal punishment. Consent never extends to independently unlawful conduct. Suspected offences involving a child will be handled in accordance with mandatory reporting and child-friendly procedures under applicable law.

12. Conduct, property and learning material

Students must treat persons, premises, equipment, books, uniforms, applications and learning material responsibly. Institute content is for the student’s personal learning and may not be commercially reproduced, redistributed or sold without written permission. Disciplinary measures will be proportionate and normally follow notice and an opportunity to respond, except for reasonable urgent restrictions needed to protect safety, records, systems or other persons.

13. Balanced responsibility

To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Institute is not responsible for indirect or consequential loss arising solely from events beyond its reasonable control, a user’s credential sharing, unauthorised third-party acts despite reasonable safeguards, or reliance on an academic guarantee not expressly given in writing. This does not exclude responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded, including responsibility for fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, breach of statutory duty, child-safety violations or unlawful handling of personal data.

14. Complaints and disputes

A concern should first be sent to the Institute’s designated grievance contact with relevant records. If unresolved, the parties may attempt good-faith mediation by mutual agreement. Subject to mandatory statutory forums and applicable law, courts at Nagpur, Maharashtra will have jurisdiction. Nothing restricts access to consumer commissions, police, child-protection authorities, data-protection authorities or another forum whose jurisdiction cannot lawfully be excluded.

15. Changes and continuing effect

If a provision is held invalid or unenforceable, it will be limited or severed only to the necessary extent. Material changes will be communicated before they apply, and fresh consent will be obtained where required. The admission offer, fee and refund schedule, privacy notice, course plan and these terms form the written understanding for the matters they cover. Mandatory law and binding government directions prevail in case of conflict.

Consent statement

By selecting the consent checkbox and signing in, the user confirms that they have read and understood these terms, had an opportunity to ask the Institute for clarification, consent to necessary processing for the stated purposes, understand that academic results are not guaranteed, and understand that optional marketing and publicity consent is not included.
