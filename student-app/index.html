Consent and portal terms

This portal processes identification, contact, enrolment, attendance, academic, examination, communication, security and related administrative records to provide Institute services.

Attendance records may include classroom entries and biometric-device identifiers and timestamps. Fingerprint images or templates are not uploaded to this ERP unless a separate notice and consent process is completed.

Academic results are not guaranteed. Accounts must not be shared. Institute rules, the written fee and refund policy, child-safety duties and rights available under applicable law continue to apply.

If the student is under 18, the person accepting these terms confirms that they are the parent or lawful guardian, or that the parent or lawful guardian has already authorised the student’s portal use.

This consent covers necessary service communications. It does not include optional promotional messages or publicity use of photographs or videos.
